# Visual do / don't

## Logo

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Use `zuri-wordmark.svg` as shipped | Retype ZURI in another face, or "fix" the tracking |
| Keep one cap-height of clear space | Crowd the wordmark against an edge or a photo subject |
| Use the amber icon for LINE avatars and app tiles | Shrink the ink-on-white wordmark into a small round avatar — it disappears |
| Invert to white on ink or on a dark photo | Recolour the wordmark to amber, or put amber on amber |
| Grab the logo straight from `assets/logos/` — already outlined | Ship anything from `assets/logos/_source/`; those are live `<text>` |
| Use the Z icon only in square contexts | Swap the Z icon in where the wordmark belongs |

## Colour

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Let amber mark the one thing that matters | Flood a layout in amber — it stops being a signal |
| Build on white / `#F7F8FA` grounds | Introduce gradients, glows or drop shadows on brand elements |
| Take hexes from `brand-profile.md` | Colour-pick from a generated image |
| Use semantic colours for state only | Use success-green or danger-red decoratively |

## Layout

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Align to a visible structure — grid, rules, dot field | Centre everything and hope |
| Wide-tracked uppercase for labels, tight for headlines | Wide-track a headline or tight-track a small label |
| Let whitespace carry the hierarchy | Fill space because it is empty |
| Show a number with what to do about it | Show a number alone |

## Mascots

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Keep Zuri competent and modern | Make her cutesy, doll-like, or a decorative receptionist |
| Keep the robot flat and geometric | Render the robot in soft 3D — it drifts to "toy" and off-brand |
| Give the two characters different jobs | Use them interchangeably, or crowd both into one small frame |
| Redraw final mascot art to `#E8820C` | Ship a raw render as final art |

## The tell

If a piece could carry another company's logo without anything looking wrong,
it is not on brand yet. The dot field, the tracked caps, the single amber signal
and the ink weight are the parts that make it zuri.
