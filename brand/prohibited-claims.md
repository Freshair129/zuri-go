# Prohibited claims

These do not ship, in any language, on any surface — including a mascot's mouth
in a LINE reply. Several are legal exposure, not style preferences.

## Accuracy and AI

| ❌ Never say | Why | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|---|
| "AI แม่นยำ 100%" / "ไม่มีวันผิดพลาด" | Unprovable, and one wrong price screenshot destroys it | "ตอบจากข้อมูลที่ร้านบันทึกไว้" |
| "AI คิดและตัดสินใจแทนคุณ" | Contradicts LOCAL-FIRST, **HUMAN-CONTROLLED** | "สรุปข้อมูลให้ คุณเป็นคนตัดสินใจ" |
| "แทนที่พนักงาน / ไม่ต้องจ้างคนเพิ่ม" | Employment claim; alienates the team who has to adopt it | "ลดงานซ้ำ ๆ ของทีม" |
| "รู้ใจลูกค้าคุณทุกคน" | Implies profiling we do not do | "เห็นประวัติการซื้อของลูกค้า" |

## Financial and performance

| ❌ Never say | Why |
|---|---|
| "ยอดขายเพิ่ม X%" without a named, dated, consented customer case | Substantiation |
| "การันตีกำไร / คืนทุนใน N เดือน" | A financial guarantee we cannot make |
| "ROI สูงสุดในตลาด" | Comparative superiority, unprovable |
| Any invented benchmark figure in creative | If a number appears, it must be traceable to a source |

Benchmarks that *are* fine, because they are general industry heuristics and are
labelled as such: `LTV ÷ CAC ≥ 3`, `CPO < gross margin per order`.

## Security, data, compliance

| ❌ Never say | Why |
|---|---|
| "ปลอดภัย 100%" / "แฮกไม่ได้" | No system claims this |
| "ผ่านมาตรฐาน ISO / SOC 2" | Only with a current certificate in hand |
| "ข้อมูลไม่ออกจากเครื่องคุณ" | Only true for genuinely local deployments — check the deployment before writing it |
| "PDPA compliant" as a blanket badge | Compliance is a posture, not a feature claim |

## Competitive and market

- No naming or depicting a competitor's product, UI, or logo
- No "อันดับ 1" / "ที่สุดในไทย" / "ผู้นำตลาด" without a citable third-party ranking
- No implying partnership or endorsement — **including LINE**. We integrate with
  LINE; we are not endorsed by LINE. Never use LINE's logo or green as ours.

## Imagery

- No fabricated dashboard screenshots showing implausible results
- No real customer name, logo, or chat content without written consent
- No stock photo presented as a zuri customer
- Any UI shown in creative must be a real state the product can actually reach

## If in doubt

Write the claim as a sentence with a source after it. If you cannot name the
source, it is not a claim — it is a wish. Cut it.
