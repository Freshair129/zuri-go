# DOM-MET — business rules

The invariants of [DOM-MET](README.md) ([STD-001 R1](../../governance/standards/STD-001-DOCUMENT-ARTIFACT-STANDARD.md): BR). Each is declared by its heading ([STD-002 R2](../../governance/standards/STD-002-IDENTITY-AND-TRACEABILITY.md)) and promoted, without changing the source, from a sentence of the “Business rules” of the domain README ([PLAN-001](../../governance/plans/PLAN-001-document-standard-adoption.md) WI-10); each quotes that sentence. All are `proposed`. The location of this file extends [STD-003 R2](../../governance/standards/STD-003-REPOSITORY-DOCUMENT-STRUCTURE.md), which names no file for a domain's rules (see the notes of this change).

### BR-009 — A weekly follower goal is net growth within the week, not a cumulative total
Relations: relates_to: FEAT-001, API-014
Owner: DOM-MET

**Status:** proposed. **Statement.** A weekly follower goal SHALL mean the net number of followers gained within the week (the latest stock minus the stock at the start of the week, for the accounts the goal names), and SHALL NOT be read as a cumulative total.

**Source.** [DOM-MET README](README.md): “A weekly follower goal means net growth within the week, not a cumulative total” ([FEAT-001 spec §1](../../features/FEAT-001-business-overview/spec.md): “เพิ่มสุทธิ 500 คนภายในสัปดาห์ … ไม่ใช่ยอดสะสมถึง 500”). **Enforced by.** the goal metric `followers_net`, measured from the `followers_total` series (`apps/api/service.mjs:save`, goals, API-014) and computed by `apps/web/src/content/business/model.mjs`.

### BR-010 — Guide definitions come from the guide cards, and graph examples are hypothetical
Relations: relates_to: FEAT-003
Owner: DOM-MET

**Status:** proposed. **Statement.** The metric guide and graph SHALL take their definitions from the guide cards, and SHALL show only hypothetical examples, never live or customer performance.

**Source.** [DOM-MET README](README.md): “Guide definitions come from the guide cards; graph examples are hypothetical and show no live performance” ([FEAT-003 feature](../../features/FEAT-003-metrics-map/feature.md): “it holds definitions and hypothetical examples, never live performance data”). **Enforced by.** the guide is static content generated by `scripts/metrics/` and served at `/metrics/`; it reads no data.

### BR-011 — NULL means unknown and 0 means confirmed zero
Relations: relates_to: ARCH-002, FEAT-001, API-015
Owner: DOM-MET

**Status:** proposed. **Statement.** An unknown value SHALL be stored and shown as unknown (`NULL`), a confirmed zero as `0`, and a missing value SHALL NOT be turned into zero before a completion or progress calculation.

**Source.** [DOM-MET README](README.md): “`NULL` means unknown and `0` means confirmed zero; a missing value is never turned into zero before a completion calculation” ([ARCH-002 §1](../../architecture/ARCH-002-postgresql-data-model.md)). **Enforced by.** `apps/api/service.mjs:observe` (a missing value is refused, never stored as 0); the Overview shows a goal without complete data as unknown (`goal.actual === null`, `summaryFacts`).

### BR-012 — Navigation and deployment changes do not change KPI definitions or formulas
Relations: relates_to: FEAT-008, FEAT-003
Owner: DOM-MET

**Status:** proposed. **Statement.** A change to the site's navigation or deployment SHALL NOT change a KPI definition, a metric formula or the owner of task data.

**Source.** [DOM-MET README](README.md): “Navigation and deployment changes do not change KPI definitions or metric formulas” ([FEAT-008 spec](../../features/FEAT-008-unified-site/spec.md): “ไม่เปลี่ยนนิยาม KPI, metric formulas หรือเจ้าของข้อมูลงาน”). **Enforced by.** review of such a change; no automated check.
